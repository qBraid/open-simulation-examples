Design a transmon, then run it

A transmon qubit with a quarter-wave readout resonator, solved as a 3D eigenmode problem in AWS Palace (open-source finite elements). The run gives the mode frequencies, losses and junction energy participations. These become a circuit Hamiltonian, which is then simulated. The colour on the metal shows where each mode's electric field sits.
